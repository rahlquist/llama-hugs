Llama Hugs persistence — updated current + proposed ERD

Current live engine: SQLite at /opt/llama-hugs/hugs.sqlite. Solid entities are current tables. Dashed yellow entities and relationships are proposed. The proposed design adds a canonical model registry, historical per-GPU smoke-test measurements, and normalized ancillary model assets. Existing live tables currently have no declared foreign keys; proposed FK lines are design intent until migration.
